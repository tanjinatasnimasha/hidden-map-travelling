A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT ASK

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I ask for help with just one service?

Yes. You can request visit or tourist visa application assistance, hotel booking, ticketing or an itinerary without arranging a full tour.

Can you arrange a trip around my requirements?

Yes. Share your interests, preferred dates, budget and travel needs. Hidden Map Travelling can arrange a tour based on your requirements, subject to availability and confirmation.

Can I request a personal tour guide?

Yes. Add the personal guide option to your trip brief. The destination, guide availability and any additional cost will need to be discussed.

Does visa assistance guarantee a visa?

No. Hidden Map Travelling assists with visit and tourist visa applications. The relevant authorities decide whether a visa is approved.

Does this planner make a booking?

No. It prepares a trip brief you can copy, download or open as an email draft. It does not take payment, issue tickets or reserve accommodation.
